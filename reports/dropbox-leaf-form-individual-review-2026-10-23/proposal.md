All 63 current originals (390,852,285 bytes) were downloaded once, matched to current Dropbox sizes/content hashes and rehashed locally. Five 12-image sheets cover all 60 individual/detail views; the sixth sheet covers the three context images. All sheets and the full-frame LF1 White050, LF2 White001, White020, White033 and In Situ002 originals were viewed. Historical maker-row37/LF2 and row36/LF1 reference images were also viewed.

LF1 has the broad leaf toward the left, converging toward the top; LF2 has the opposite orientation. The 21 horizontal pictures depict the shared pattern. Their photographs alone do not establish a unique LF1/LF2 association or physical cast identity, so the proposal keeps their originals in a shared Horizontal Views folder. Close-ups showing an identifiable upright pattern remain with that numbered orientation and retain their detail role.

The plan retains the old 60-image source folder ID for Leaf Form 2 White (31 images), creates Leaf Form 1 White (8) and Leaf Form White Horizontal Views (21), and moves exactly 29 originals into those new direct child folders. It renames the three-image context folder to Leaf Form 1 White In Situ. Original filenames stay unchanged.

Existing arrangement folders, seven Gold images and the empty Members Unclassified folder remain unchanged. LF1/LF2 remain orientation choices of one canonical design; the folder split creates no new catalogue design/option/mould/stock identities. All source/finish/context qualifications remain in place.
