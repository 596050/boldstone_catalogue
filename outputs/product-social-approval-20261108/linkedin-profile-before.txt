											93 text I make bespoke wall sculpture, relief panels, water features and sculptural furnishings for interiors, gardens and architectural settings - cast and carved in plaster, marble and stone composites, finished in white marble, verdigris, gold leaf, bronze and hand-painted effects. I grew up in South Africa and studied Anthropology, Fine Art and History of Art at the University of the Witwatersrand, then spent twelve years in museums: first the Windhoek Museum in Namibia, later the South African Museum of Natural History and Anthropology in Cape Town, where I designed and ran the Discovery Room. That background still shapes the work. The designs come from pattern in nature - the corrugations of sand ripples, the curves and spirals of waves and shells, the shifting shadow shapes of the moon's phases, the geometry of plants, snowflakes, crystals and sea creatures. I cast carved shapes into wall panels, setting the hard edge of the block against the organic form held within it. That method has become Boldstone's signature. I began making wall sculptures in Cape Town in 1993 and founded Boldstone Sculpture in the UK in 1998. Since then I have worked with architects, landscape architects, interior designers and garden designers, as well as private clients, throughout Britain and Europe and further afield in the USA and the Middle East. Pieces are placed on walls in private and corporate gardens and interiors, or made up as water features and paving. Commissions are welcome at any scale, finish or specification, and samples can be supplied. Trade and commission enquiries: info@boldstonesculpture.com boldstonesculpture.com
