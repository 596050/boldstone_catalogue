																	88 text entry area (settable) WHO IS BEHIND BOLDSTONE SCULPTURE?
Boldstone Sculpture was founded in 1998 by British sculptor Sally Price, who creates sculptures, furnishings, contemporary reliefs, wall panels and water features for homes, gardens and commercial spaces.
DO YOU OFFER READY‑MADE PIECES AS WELL AS COMMISSIONS?
Yes. Standard catalogue wall sculptures are ready to dispatch within 10 working days of order confirmation, unless a different deadline is clearly agreed. Bespoke commissions and large projects have a separately agreed production and dispatch schedule. Carrier transit time is additional.
WHAT MATERIALS DO YOU USE?
Catalogue wall sculptures are made from a gypsum-based material. Colour effects are applied as surface finishes, rather than solid metal. Each sculpture is handmade, so the pattern and surface effect may vary from the photograph. Sally Price can discuss alternative finishes and bespoke commissions.
CAN THE SCULPTURES BE INSTALLED OUTDOORS?
Absolutely. Many pieces are designed for exterior walls, courtyards and landscaped gardens. Outdoor finishes are weather‑resistant; the team will advise on fixings and maintenance for your specific site.
HOW HEAVY ARE THE PANELS?
Reliefs can be cast hollow or solid to suit your wall type and weight limits. During consultation, Boldstone will confirm exact weights and recommend suitable fixings.
WHICH COUNTRIES DO YOU SHIP TO?
We arrange UK and international delivery. Eligible UK delivery charges are shown at checkout. For international destinations or orders unavailable at checkout, contact info@boldstone.co.uk for a confirmed shipping quote before ordering.
HOW ARE SHIPPING COSTS CALCULATED?
Delivery depends on the fully packed size, weight and destination. Eligible charges appear before payment at checkout. Large, heavy, specially packed and international orders may need a separately confirmed quote.
WILL I HAVE TO PAY IMPORT DUTIES OR TAXES?
The confirmed international shipping quotation will explain any applicable import duties, taxes and packing charges for the chosen service.
HOW LONG WILL DELIVERY TAKE ONCE MY PIECE IS READY?
Standard catalogue wall sculptures are ready to dispatch within 10 working days of order confirmation, unless otherwise agreed. Working days exclude weekends and UK public holidays. Carrier transit time is additional and varies by service and destination.
CAN I TRACK MY ORDER?
Yes. You will receive an email with tracking details as soon as your parcel leaves the studio. If you have not received an update, contact Boldstone with your order number.
WHAT IS YOUR RETURN POLICY?
For standard catalogue sculptures bought online, contact us within 30 days of delivery to request a change-of-mind return, then send the sculpture back within 14 days of notifying us. You pay the actual return postage. You may inspect it as you would in a shop. Bespoke or personalised commissions may be excluded from change-of-mind returns when identified before ordering. Standard catalogue designs and sale items retain statutory consumer rights.
HOW DO I START A RETURN?
Email info@boldstone.co.uk with your order number. We will provide the return address and packing instructions. For damaged, faulty or incorrect items, contact us promptly so we can arrange the appropriate remedy and necessary return postage.
WHEN WILL I RECEIVE MY REFUND?
We refund the original payment method within 14 days of receiving the returned sculpture or evidence that it has been sent back, whichever comes first. Qualifying cancellations include the standard outbound delivery charge. A bank or card provider may take additional time to display the credit. See the Return and Refund Policy for full details.
DO YOU PROVIDE INSTALLATION OR PROJECT‑MANAGEMENT SUPPORT?
Yes. The studio can liaise with architects, landscape designers and contractors, advise on fixings, and oversee installation to ensure the artwork integrates seamlessly with your space.
HOW CAN I DISCUSS A PROJECT OR GET A QUOTE?
Use the contact form on the Contact Us page or email info@boldstonesculpture.com. You can also schedule a consultation call directly from the website.
