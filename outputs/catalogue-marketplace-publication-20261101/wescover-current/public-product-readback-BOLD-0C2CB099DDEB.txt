A spiral motif gathers the eye into a textured, organic relief. The design suggests the shapes and rhythms of the coast, bringing a touch of the underwater world to the wall.

Its changing shadows add depth without needing a busy setting; give the sculpture a little space and let the form speak.

The white finish keeps the focus on the form and its changing shadows.
