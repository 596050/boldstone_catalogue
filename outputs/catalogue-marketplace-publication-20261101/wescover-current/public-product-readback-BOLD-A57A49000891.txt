A moonshadow-inspired form creates a calm relief of curve and depth. Light moves across its contours, revealing a quiet balance of highlight and shade.

It evokes the moment when a quiet tide meets the night sky, bringing a restful rhythm to a considered wall. This listing is for one component; the companion piece is available separately.

The white finish keeps the focus on the form and its changing shadows.
