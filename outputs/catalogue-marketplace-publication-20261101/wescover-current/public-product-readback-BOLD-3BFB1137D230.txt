A flowing relief gives this fountain design a sculptural presence. Its contours invite light and shadow to move across the surface, creating a focal point with a calm, architectural character.

Imagine a secluded courtyard where a considered piece of sculpture gives the whole space a sense of balance. This is the water-feature version; please contact us to discuss the installation and water-system requirements.

The white finish keeps the focus on the form and its changing shadows.
