Concentric lines ripple across the surface in a pattern reminiscent of a fingerprint. The familiar becomes abstract, with repeated ridges giving the relief a gentle, absorbing rhythm.

It is an invitation to slow down and notice small details: the trace of a line, the space between rings and the way light moves across them.

The cream finish brings a soft, warm tone to the design.
