A wave motif becomes a rhythmic, decorative relief, balancing flowing curves with a clear, graphic shape. Light and shadow give its surface movement throughout the day.

The feeling is coastal with an Art Deco inflection: a small escape into the rhythm of the sea.

A verdigris-effect finish adds sea-green colour and a weathered visual character.
