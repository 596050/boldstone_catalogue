Folded forms bring a sense of movement to the relief, with ridges and recesses creating a gentle visual rhythm. The design explores the meeting of a crisp edge and a flowing curve.

A piece that changes with the light, recalling the quiet choreography of a fan opening or a wing unfolding.

The white finish keeps the focus on the form and its changing shadows.
