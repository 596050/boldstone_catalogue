A single botanical form opens across this relief, with broad, flowing curves that give the flower its sculptural presence. The design keeps the details simple so that light and shadow can follow the contours.

Think sunlit courtyard walls and the graceful lines of classical foliage, translated into a piece that feels at home in a modern space.

The white finish keeps the focus on the form and its changing shadows.
