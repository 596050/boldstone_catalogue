A shell-inspired form meets a lunar curve in a relief with flowing, organic movement. The raised contours gather light, while recesses create a softer rhythm of shadows.

Its mood sits between shoreline and night sky, making a restful focal point for a garden or interior. This is the water-feature version; please contact us to discuss the installation and water-system requirements.

The white finish keeps the focus on the form and its changing shadows.
