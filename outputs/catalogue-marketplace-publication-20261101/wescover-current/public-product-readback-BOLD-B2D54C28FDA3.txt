Flowing lines and a sculpted rhythm bring together the movement of dunes and an Art Deco sensibility. The relief catches light along its contours, giving a still wall a sense of motion.

A design for spaces that balance clean lines with natural forms, from a bright interior to a planted courtyard.

A verdigris-effect finish adds sea-green colour and a weathered visual character.
