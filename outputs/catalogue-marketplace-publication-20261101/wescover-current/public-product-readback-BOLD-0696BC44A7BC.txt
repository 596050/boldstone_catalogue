A broad rim frames a crater-inspired form, giving this relief a strong outline and a more intimate centre. The contrast between edge and surface detail makes the changing light part of the design.

It suggests a weathered landscape seen from above, where shape, colour and shadow come together.

This version is offered in the rust drip colourway.
