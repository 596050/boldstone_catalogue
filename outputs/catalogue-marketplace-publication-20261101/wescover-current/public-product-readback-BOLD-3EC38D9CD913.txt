A spiral winds through a moon-inspired relief, drawing the eye around the form. The raised contours catch the light while the recesses lend depth and quiet contrast.

It offers a gentle sense of movement, like a tide or orbit held in a single sculptural gesture.

The white finish keeps the focus on the form and its changing shadows.
