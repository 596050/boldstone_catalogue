Rippling contours recall the patterns left by wind and water across sand. The relief turns that fleeting natural rhythm into a sculptural composition, with highlights and shadows following every change of direction.

Think of a shoreline at low tide, when the smallest ridges become a landscape of their own.

This version is offered in the bronze and verdigris colourway.
