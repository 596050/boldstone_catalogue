20 September 2026
      To maintain service reliability and secure the additional resources required to support our
      customers, we are implementing the Surge Emergency Fee and Demand Surcharge, which will
      take effect on 20 September 2026 and will remain in place until 6 February 2027.

      Surge Emergency Fee (Domestic)

               •   A Surge Emergency Fee1 of €0.55 per package (or the equivalent in local currency) will
                   apply to domestic shipments (domestic Standard, Express, Express Saver, Express
                   Plus), delivered to residential addresses. In Italy, this fee will apply to both residential
                   and commercial deliveries.

      Surge Emergency Fee (International Ground)

               •   A Surge Emergency Fee of €0.15 per kilogram (or the equivalent in local currency) will
                   apply to worldwide and transborder UPS Standard shipments delivered to residential
                   addresses.
