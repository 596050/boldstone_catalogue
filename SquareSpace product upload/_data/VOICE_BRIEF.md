# Boldstone — article voice brief

## Who makes this work
Boldstone Sculpture is a small British studio. Every piece is individually carved and cast by
hand, so no two are identical. The recurring subject is **natural form** — ammonites, spirals,
moons, waves, dunes, shells, seed heads, bone structure — rendered in relief and finished in
white marble stone, verdigris, bronze or gold leaf. The prints and cards come from the same
eye: close, patient observation of natural history.

## Voice
- Quiet, confident, unhurried. A maker talking about the work, not a brochure shouting.
- Concrete over abstract. Name the material, the process, the reference in nature.
- Sentences of varied length. Short ones land hardest. Use them.
- No hype. Never "stunning", "gorgeous", "must-have", "elevate your space", "timeless elegance".
- No emoji. No exclamation marks.
- British spelling: colour, verdigris, sculpted, moulded, textured, jewellery.

## What each article must do
Write **500–1000 words** of genuinely specific prose about **this one piece**. It must not be
interchangeable with any other article. Anchor it in the actual facts you are given: the exact
dimensions, price, material, finish, subject and edition.

Suggested shape (vary it — do not use the same headings every time):
1. **Open with the object.** What it is, what it looks like, what the eye does first.
2. **The natural reference.** The ammonite, the moon phase, the dune ripple, the seed head —
   what it is in nature, why it recurs in art and design, why it is worth looking at. This is
   where you can be genuinely interesting: real natural history, real design history.
3. **Making it.** Carved then cast. What the finish does to the light. Why relief is a medium
   about shadow, not colour — the piece changes through the day as the light moves.
4. **Living with it.** Where it works, what it needs (wall, light, space), how it sits with
   other things. Practical, not prescriptive.
5. **The specifics.** Dimensions, finish, price, that it is made to order / one of a kind.
6. **Close with a link** to the product page.

## Facts you may rely on
- Carved by hand, then cast. Marble stone finish unless the description says otherwise.
- Suitable for interior or garden/exterior walls where the description says so.
- Made in Britain, in-studio.
- Available in other sizes and finishes on request (for sculptures) — contact the studio.

## Facts you must NOT invent
Do not invent an artist's biography, awards, exhibitions, press coverage, collector names,
dates, or a founding story. Do not invent materials or dimensions not given to you. If a fact
is not in the product data or the list above, leave it out. Natural history and design history
are fair game — that is general knowledge, not a claim about Boldstone.

## Format
Markdown. Front matter exactly as below, then the article.

```
---
title: "<product title>"
slug: <slug>
product_url: <url>
price: "£<price>"
type: <type>
tags: [<3–6 of the product's tags>]
meta_description: "<150–158 characters, written for search, mentioning the piece by name>"
---

# <An article headline — NOT just the product name. Make it about the idea.>

<the article>
```

End every article with a final line linking the product:

`**<Title>** — <dimensions>, <finish>. [View the piece](<url>)`
