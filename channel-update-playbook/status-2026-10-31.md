## Test cleanup and limits

The single test basket item was removed, and the basket was verified empty. Performance/analytics and advertising cookie choices were restored to their prior off state after testing. Test mode was not enabled and no purchase event was fabricated. The earlier test-mode proposal was superseded after confirming that native Meta purchase events are suppressed for test orders; use a genuine completed order for purchase verification.

The Squarespace Orders page, with **All Statuses** selected and no search/filter applied, showed **No orders yet**. There was no existing store order available to reconcile in this run.

The inspected Meta Google-reporting connection control was disabled. Chrome's ad-blocking extension logged storage errors, but these do not establish the cause of missing Meta test events. No extension or security setting was changed.

Meta's sampled-activity UI warns that custom data may be shown as `_removed_`. It exposed the product URLs and the presence of `contents`/`content_ids`, but the exact sample values were not verified. Seeing a tracked URL in an event is useful route evidence; it is not purchase attribution or a passed catalogue-match test.

Website orders use Stripe connected as **info@boldstone.co.uk**. The payout bank account was not inspected. The Marketplace flow used here did not expose an integrated payment/payout setup; a Marketplace ad/payment-method screen is not proof of where customer payments settle. Buyers following the website link use the website checkout; other arrangements must be agreed directly.
