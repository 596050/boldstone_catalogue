# Current wall-sculpture publishing instructions

Owner instruction, 1 November 2026. This is the current rule for all wall sculptures and supersedes the 31 October instruction to hide the base material.

- Add a separate description point: **Material: Made from a gypsum-based material.**
- Where the component has an applied decorative finish, add **Surface effect: A decorative surface effect is applied to create the finish shown.** Retain the accurate named finish in its design copy. For gold-leaf finishes use imitation gold leaf unless the component explicitly records a different owner-approved finish.
- A colour/metal/stone name describes appearance, not a solid bronze, copper, gold, marble or natural-stone construction. Do not introduce a brand, recipe, certification or safety claim that the catalogue does not establish.
- Retain current retail names, dimensions, prices, selected photographs, availability and lead time. A description-only update does not recalculate the manufacturing scenario, labour or markup.
- Keep the existing outdoor-use and keyhole/French-cleat wording unless the owner changes it. Keep detailed postage tariffs out of descriptions.
