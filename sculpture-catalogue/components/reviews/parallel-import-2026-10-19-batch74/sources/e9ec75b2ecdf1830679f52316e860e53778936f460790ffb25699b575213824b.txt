Customisation
 We specialise in flexibility and
 originality.Whether you have a
 specific design to be brought to life
 or would like us to create
 something entirely new, we are
 here to fulfil your vision.

 Examples of materials we've
 worked with include:
   - Stone composites (any colour)
   - Concrete (any colour)
   - Gypsum (any colour)
   - Liquid metals (any effect)
   - Metal leaf and gilding
   - Resins (any colour)
   - Paints
 Materials can be finished to high
 gloss, satin sheen or matte, with
 varying textures.

 We collaborate with partners to
 deliver diverse projects. Upon
 request, we can provide details of
 our trusted network of suppliers
 and specialist organisations,
 ensuring outstanding results.
Lighting
 We craft pieces from premium
 composite materials to receive
 lighting, selected for durability
 indoors and outdoors.We have
 worked with engineers to integrate
 LED solutions, providing longevity,
 superior energy efficiency,
 excellent colour rendering,
 adjustable colour temperatures,
 RGB options, and dimming
 controls.We have made sconces,
 lamps and backlighting, we can
 alter designs according to lighting
 requirements.

 Wall fixings are set into the piece,
 with Keyhole and French Cleat
 fixing systems as default
 options.We supply instructions for
 care and maintenance of all
 works.

 Mock-ups, and technical
 drawings can be provided on
 request.

Bespoke
 Each commission begins with a
 dialogue - whether you're starting
 with an idea or seeking conceptual
 guidance, we work closely with
 you to define objectives and
 possibilities.

 Our bespoke process unfolds in
 four key stages: planning, where
 we explore goals and context
 through consultation and material
 research, defining the expected
 outcome; refinement, where
 ideas are developed into detailed
 proposals and responsibilities are
 assigned; quoting, where
 transparent costings cover all
 aspects of production and
 installation; and execution, where
 our skilled team brings the vision
 to life with precision and
 craftsmanship in collaboration with
 stakeholders.
Ethos
 Our sculptural forms are inspired
 by the timeless rhythms of nature.
 We strive to elevate individuality
 and instil a tactile sense of place
 through our designs.

 We create wall pieces, water
 features, tables, lighting, and
 custom furnishings designed to
 harmonise with environments and
 fulfil aspirations.

 Our principles:
 Each project uniquely reflects our
 clients’ objectives.
 Designs that elegantly unite
 history, aesthetics, and
 functionality.
 Client-centred approach -
 ensuring every step feels intuitive,
 personal, and meticulously
 guided.
Archetypes
 We believe in creating elemental
 shapes that embody fundamental
 emotions. Our work transforms
 feelings into physical expressions,
 blending the essence of organic
 forms with geometric structures to
 meet each client’s unique needs.

 For example, we might begin by
 discussing concepts like Sand &
 Sea, which evoke tranquility and
 the gentle movement of wind
 over sand. Through our design
 process, we distil these ideas into
 visual representations that capture
 their emotional resonance,
 resulting in sculptural pieces that
 bring both aesthetic harmony
 and meaningful connection to a
 space.
Riyadh, Saudi Arabia
 A palace in Riyadh required
 completely new designs with
 architectural scale.In addition to
 coordinating internationally and
 across multiple partners:

 Custom Design and
 Craftsmanship - We designed,
 carved, and cast 18 large, curved
 panels (275cm x 100cm each)
 featuring abstract desert-inspired
 elements such as dune ripples and
 shell forms.

 Innovative Lighting Solutions -
 Integrated backlighting with
 overlapping curved panels,
 intensifying the visual impact of the
 subtle relief carvings.

 Textural Excellence - To evoke
 the essence of the desert
 environment, a textured sand
 surface was applied to each panel,
 delivering an authentic tactile and
 visual experience.
Ivory House, UK
 For the historic Ivory House
 entrance, we crafted a 6 x 5 metre
 sculpture, our focal points for the
 project were:

 Authenticity - Incorporating the
 original London Stock bricks as the
 backdrop, we honoured the
 building's history and character.

 Artistic Detail - Cast panels
 vividly captured impressions of
 ostrich feathers, spices, and
 scallop shells, directly referencing
 the building's trade heritage.

 Innovative Design - We
 integrated repainted wooden
 patterns and dynamic LED
 lighting, creating a visually
 captivating, floating effect that
 highlighted the blend of tradition
 with contemporary design.
Palace Terrace, UK
 For a large terraced house
 refurbishment project, a private
 residence in Kensington Palace
 Gardens, required significant
 technical engagement, our
 priorities were:

 Custom Fabrication -
 Collaborating closely with clients,
 designers, and engineers, we
 designed and precisely
 manufactured smooth, flat panels
 and a striking 2 x 1 metre sculpted
 panel named ‘Icarus’.

 Innovative Lighting Integration -
  Each artwork featured embedded
 LED backlighting, expertly
 engineered to produce an elegant
 floating appearance on the wall.

 Reliable Installation Solutions -
 Utilising a robust French cleat
 system for secure and seamless
 mounting, ensuring both aesthetic
 appeal and structural integrity.
