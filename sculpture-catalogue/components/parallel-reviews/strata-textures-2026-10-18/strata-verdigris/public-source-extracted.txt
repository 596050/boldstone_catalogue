Strata in Verdigris
£250.00
Strata in Verdigris is a handmade wall sculpture of stacked, layered ledges bands of shifting relief that recall sedimentary rock or a cliff face worn back to its layers. Each form is carved and cast by hand in the Boldstone studio in Bath, England, then finished with a hand-applied faux verdigris patina, shifting from deep sea-green in the recesses to pale chalky turquoise along the raised edges. Because the patina is worked by hand, no two pieces take the finish in quite the same way.
Cast gypsum composite with a hand-applied faux verdigris finish. Measures 30 × 30 × 6 cm and weighs 4 kg, hangs flush to the wall and arrives ready to hang. The square format suits a hallway, stairwell, bathroom or a quiet wall in a living room, and groups beautifully with other sculptures in the range for a larger installation.
Made in Bath, England by sculptor Sally Price of Boldstone Sculpture. Textured, tactile contemporary wall art in verdigris green a sculptural alternative to framed art for interior designers, architects and collectors.
