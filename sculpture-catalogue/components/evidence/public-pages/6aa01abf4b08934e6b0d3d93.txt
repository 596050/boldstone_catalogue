Bronze Surface Wall Sculpture Home Decor Sea Theme Rising Sun & Waves Relief

This striking wall sculpture features a raised relief of concentric radiating arcs - a bold rising sun and wave motif inspired by the sea. Finished in warm bronze and brown surface effect, the sculpted curves create real depth and shadow, catching the light beautifully across the piece.
Measuring 30 cm x 30 cm x 6 cm, the sculpture is crafted from plaster with a smooth, contoured relief on a square panel. It is ready to hang, with a recessed fixing on the back, and makes a distinctive focal point.
Its abstract, nature-inspired design suits any room - ideal above a console, in a hallway or entryway, a living room, or a garden room or courtyard. A stylish, one-of-a-kind piece that is sure to be a conversation starter.
