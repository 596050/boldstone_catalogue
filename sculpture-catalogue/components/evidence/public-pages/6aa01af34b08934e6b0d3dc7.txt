Handmade Blue & White Marbled Abstract Wall Art Panel Tall Narrow
£188.00
A striking one-off wall art panel, hand-marbled in a swirl of cobalt and cornflower blue with cream, white and soft grey, broken up by fine charcoal flecks. The poured pattern is worked into a raised, rippling relief, so the surface catches the light and looks different from every angle.
Every panel is marbled by hand, so no two are ever the same and this piece is a genuine one-off.
The tall, narrow format is ideal for the awkward spaces that are hardest to fill: a hallway, the wall beside a door or staircase, a chimney breast, or the gap between two windows. It can be hung vertically or horizontally to suit your space.
The cool blue and cream palette sits easily with white, grey and natural wood interiors, and as the photos show it looks just as good against exposed stone or a garden wall.
The side edges are finished in the same marbled pattern as the face, so the panel looks good from every angle.
Please note this is a handmade piece, so small variations in texture and pattern are part of its character.
