Barbados Wave
£2,880.00
This large wall sculpture takes the form of a breaking wave - a deep spiral scroll curling inward at the centre, with the edge of the panel lifting and folding over like the crest of the wave itself. Cast in Bath stone Jesmonite, the carved form throws real depth and shadow, and reads differently as the light moves across it through the day.
Measuring 100 cm x 100 cm x 8 cm and weighing approximately 83 kg, this is a substantial architectural piece. It is delivered on a pallet, and given the weight, professional installation onto a solid wall is recommended.
Shown here on a terrace wall beside a pool, it suits any setting with the scale to carry it - a courtyard or garden wall, a hotel lobby, a double-height hallway or a stairwell. Handmade to order by Boldstone in Bath.
