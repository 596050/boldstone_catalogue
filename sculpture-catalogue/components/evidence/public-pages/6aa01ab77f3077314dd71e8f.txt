Wall sculpture with a wave relief and hand-painted faux rust-on-iron
£60.33
This striking wall sculpture features a flowing wave relief with sculpted scroll and spiral detailing, finished in a hand-painted faux rust-on-iron effect that gives it a warm, weathered, industrial-meets-coastal character. The rich rusty-brown tones and textured surface catch the light beautifully and create real depth and movement across the piece.
Measuring 35 cm x 21 cm x 6 cm, the sculpture has a raised three-dimensional relief on a rectangular panel and is ready to hang, with a fixing recessed into the back. The durable textured finish makes it suitable for both indoor and outdoor display.
Its abstract, nature-inspired wave design brings a bold artistic focal point to any space - ideal above a console, in a hallway or entryway, in a garden room or courtyard, or anywhere that needs a tactile, eye-catching feature. A distinctive piece that is sure to be a conversation starter.
