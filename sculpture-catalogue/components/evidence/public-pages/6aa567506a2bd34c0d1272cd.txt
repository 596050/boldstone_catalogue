Acanthus Full Wall Sculpture Curved Relief White
£175.00
This striking wall sculpture features smooth, curved relief forms - overlapping acanthus leaf blades that sweep across the panel. Finished in a pure white surface, the sculpted curves create real depth and shadow, catching the light beautifully across the piece.
Measuring 50 cm x 30 cm x 6 cm and weighing approximately 8 kg, the sculpture is crafted from plaster with a smooth, contoured relief on a rectangular panel. It is ready to hang, with a recessed fixing on the back, and makes a distinctive focal point.
Its nature-inspired acanthus design suits any room - ideal above a console, in a hallway or entryway, a living room, or a garden room or courtyard. A stylish, one-of-a-kind piece that is sure to be a conversation starter.
